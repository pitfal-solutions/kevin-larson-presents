# Buyers — the KLP owners

**Who:** The owners/operators of Kevin Larson Presents. They've run this
events business for 30 years and currently maintain 6 separate WordPress
sites themselves.

**What they're purchasing:** A website facelift, and potentially follow-on
social media / marketing enhancements (upsell, not v1 scope).

**What they're judging the demo on:**

- Does this look like a *step up* from what they have, immediately, on
  first glance? They already have a strong dark-luxury visual identity —
  the bar is "better than good," not "different."
- Does it capture the energy of their events (VIP, exclusive, a little
  theatrical) without looking like a template?
- Is their real content (event names, dates, venues, taglines) represented
  accurately? A demo that gets their own event details wrong undermines
  trust immediately.
- Does it work on their phone, in front of them, without them having to
  squint or pinch-zoom — most of their audience will be booking events via
  mobile.

**Pain we're solving for them:** current site is "old and clunky" and
doesn't build excitement the way their actual events do — no showcase of
past events, disjointed multi-domain structure, unclear how findable it is
to people (and increasingly, AI assistants) searching for "Denver Halloween
party" or similar.

**How to pitch:** a link they can open on their own phone, showing their own
event copy done right — not a generic template with lorem ipsum.
