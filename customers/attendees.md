# Attendees — the people who buy tickets

**Who:** The audience the *website itself* has to convert — people deciding
whether to buy a ticket to a KLP event. Not our direct customer (KLP is),
but the page has to work for them or it fails the buyer's actual goal.

**Who they are, per the current sites:** 21+ Denver-area adults looking for
a "once in a lifetime" night out — NYE, Halloween, Derby Day, Mardi Gras,
girls'-night/pajama-party crowd for Jammy Jam. Willing to pay for a premium/
VIP experience, often booking with a group (referral program rewards
bringing friends).

**What convinces them, per existing copy and structure:**

- Concrete, glamorous specifics: venue name (Ritz-Carlton, The Church),
  exact theme, what's included (open bar, private areas, costume contest
  prize amount).
- Proof it's a real, established, well-produced event — "30 years,"
  multiple signature nights, professional photography/performers.
- **Seeing what past events actually looked like.** This is the single
  biggest gap in the current site per the client's stated pain — "showcase
  ...past events to get people excited to attend." Nothing sells a themed
  party like photos of last year's version. v1 prioritizes this.
- Clear, fast path to buy (ticket link) and a reason to act now (referral
  program, "sale ends," discounted hotel rooms going fast).

**Implication for copy/design:** don't soften the existing voice — it's
already speaking their language ("Dress to impress — and dress to win").
Elevate execution, don't genericize the tone.
