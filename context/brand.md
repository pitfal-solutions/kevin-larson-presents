# Brand

Extracted directly from `kevinlarsonpresents.com` and its event microsites on
2026-08-22 (see [data-sources.md](data-sources.md)). This is what the *new*
design should carry forward and elevate — not invent from scratch.

## Voice

- Primary tagline: **"Live Passionately, Experience Extraordinary"**
- Mission line: **"Creating Immersive Experiences in Denver"**
- Recurring philosophy line: **"When exceptional people gather, the ordinary
  disappears"**
- A recurring brand motif called the **"Flame of Passion"** — copy that
  leans into intensity/transformation ("Light your fire. Burn brighter. Be
  the spark. Be the flame. Be the passion.")
- Per-event lines are punchy and specific, e.g. "NOLA stops. Denver
  doesn't!", "Dress to impress — and dress to win," "This isn't a venue.
  It's a takeover."
- Tone: confident, a little theatrical, exclusive-but-inviting. Not corporate.

## Visual identity (current site)

- Background: near-black, `rgb(16,16,21)`
- Body text: cool light gray, `rgb(153,166,177)`
- Headings: white, serif — **Libre Baskerville**
- Body/UI: sans-serif — **Poppins**
- Accent: warm gold/orange flame mark in the logo; gold/black pill-shaped CTA
  buttons ("GET TICKETS")
- Logo: a stylized flame icon beside "Kevin Larson Presents" in script/serif

## What "upgrade" means here

The current site already has a strong dark-luxury identity — the gap isn't
the color palette, it's:

- **Fragmentation** — 6 separate WordPress sites/domains for one brand, no
  unified story or cross-linking that feels intentional.
- **Dated execution** — visual/interaction quality doesn't match the "$2K
  costume contest, Ritz-Carlton, VIP everything" positioning.
- **No visible past-event proof** — nothing on the current homepage builds
  FOMO from what already happened (see [../customers/attendees.md](../customers/attendees.md)
  on why that matters to the buyer).
- **Not AI/SEO structured** — see [ai-discoverability.md](ai-discoverability.md).

v1 keeps the existing palette/fonts/voice (it's good) and raises execution
quality, adds a past-events section, and adds structure an AI agent or search
engine can actually parse.
