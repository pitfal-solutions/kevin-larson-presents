# Product

## What Kevin Larson Presents is

A 30-year-old Denver live-events producer. Kevin Larson Presents (KLP) runs
large, ticketed, themed "hotel takeover" parties — not weddings, not private
bookings. Think: a whole hotel or venue rented out and transformed around a
theme, with DJs/live music, roaming performers, multiple bars, and a VIP tier.

## The five signature events (as of the current live site)

| Event | Theme | Venue (recent/upcoming) | Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| White Rose Gala | NYE, Gatsby/1920s | The Ritz-Carlton Denver | Dec 31 |
| Denver Mardi Gras | NOLA hotel takeover | DoubleTree by Hilton DTC | Feb 27 |
| Denver Derby Day | Kentucky Derby watch party | The Ritz-Carlton Denver | May 1 |
| Denver Halloween ("Paranormal Palace") | Costume contest, haunted decor | DoubleTree by Hilton DTC | Oct 31 |
| Jammy Jam | Grown-up pajama party, women-focused | The Church (Denver) | Sept 19 |

Common ingredients across events: 21+, hotel/venue takeover so guests "ride
the elevator home," multiple DJs and/or live music, roaming/theatrical
performers, a paid VIP tier (open bar, private areas, faster lines), a
referral program ("refer 8 friends, ticket's free"), and hotel-room booking
add-ons.

## What's being upgraded (this project)

Not the events business itself — the **web presence**. Today that's 6
separate WordPress sites (main site + one microsite per event) with
inconsistent structure. This project's Current Goal (per the client) is a
single, modern, high-end landing page that:

- Showcases the signature events (upcoming *and* past, to build excitement)
  at a "top of the line" design bar — see [../customers/buyers.md](../customers/buyers.md)
  for exactly who's judging that bar.
- Reads as premium/nightlife/luxury, matching the brand voice already
  established (see [brand.md](brand.md)).
- Is AI- and SEO-friendly by construction — see
  [ai-discoverability.md](ai-discoverability.md).
